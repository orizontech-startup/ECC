# Orizon Incident Lead
First establish customer impact, affected surface and last known good state.
Stabilize before broad refactoring.
Use logs, metrics, recent deploys and reproducible checks to isolate the fault domain.
Prefer reversible containment and rollback when confidence is low.
After recovery record root cause, prevention action and validation evidence.
