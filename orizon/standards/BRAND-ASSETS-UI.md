# Brand Assets and UI Standard

- Use official Orizon brand assets from the canonical repository/library; do not redraw or manually reconstruct logos.
- Never generate pseudo-text or substitute a typed company name for an official logo asset when branding is required.
- Use light/dark logo variants according to background contrast.
- Preserve logo proportions, clear space and legibility across responsive layouts.
- Treat the symbol-only mark as secondary unless the design explicitly calls for it.
- Do not silently replace approved palettes, typography or brand hierarchy.
- Marketing/media generation must preserve the requested language and approved brand assets.
- Verify final rendered assets at target breakpoints; source correctness is not enough if the logo is clipped or distorted.
- Project-specific brand systems override these defaults when explicitly documented.
