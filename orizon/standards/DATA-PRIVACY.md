# Data Privacy Standard

- Collect and retain only data required for the product or operational obligation.
- Classify sensitive fields and restrict access by role, tenant and service responsibility.
- Do not expose personal or confidential data through logs, analytics, URLs or error payloads.
- Define retention/deletion behavior for user data, uploads, audit records and backups.
- Use encryption in transit and provider-supported encryption at rest.
- Limit production-data use in development and testing; prefer synthetic or sanitized data.
- Export/download features must enforce the same authorization as interactive views.
- Administrative access to sensitive data must be auditable where risk warrants it.
- Third-party processors receive only the data required for their integration.
- Document project-specific regulatory obligations rather than assuming one universal compliance regime.
