# AI Media & Branding

Use this standard for generated marketing images, video, copy and branded creative.

- Official brand assets are source material; do not reconstruct logos with generated text.
- Never manually typeset the company name as a substitute for the official logo asset.
- Choose approved light/dark logo variants based on background contrast.
- Treat pseudo-text, misspelled brand names and altered marks as generation defects.
- Keep prompts explicit about language, aspect ratio, safe margins and text restrictions.
- Prefer deterministic composition of brand assets after generation when model-rendered logos are unreliable.
- Validate every generated asset visually before publication or campaign approval.
- Keep copy and creative generation separate from final publishing so failed publishing can be retried without regenerating content.
- Record the provider/model used for reproducibility and cost/latency analysis.
- Optimize generation pipelines for product latency without weakening quality gates.
