# Vendor & Cost Governance

Use this standard for recurring SaaS, cloud, AI, database and infrastructure costs.

- Identify the production-critical vendors and who owns each account.
- Prefer one intentional service per capability; remove accidental duplicate subscriptions.
- Track free-tier limits, hard quotas, overage behavior and billing alerts before production scale.
- Separate fixed recurring cost from usage-variable cost.
- Model small, medium and large operating ranges for pricing and margin decisions.
- Avoid architectural lock-in where a provider abstraction is inexpensive and strategically useful.
- Record renewal dates, payment owners and critical service dependencies.
- Monitor AI/token/media-generation spend by feature when it can materially affect gross margin.
- Treat cost optimization as a production constraint, not a reason to remove required reliability or security.
- Review vendor spend at major scale changes and before customer pricing changes.
